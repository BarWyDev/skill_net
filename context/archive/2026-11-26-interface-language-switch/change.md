## Notes

Roadmap S-18, FR-021. A visitor can switch the interface between Polish, English and Ukrainian with a switcher in the header; the first visit follows the browser's language, the choice is remembered in a cookie, and the landing page, the public map, sign-in, sign-up, the confirmation pages and the error pages follow it, at the same addresses. This slice carries the message catalogues and the language resolution that S-19, S-20 and S-21 build on.
